    SECONDARY REFERENCE

    Make Every Hit Count

    W A S D Move on the arena plane

    E Jump / ascend. Double-E is a flight alias.

    E + Q / C + Q Air dodge up / down. Shift remains an alternate for Q.

    Q / Shift Dodge in your movement direction

    Q + Arrows Evade left / right / forward / back, without turning the camera. Either key order; Shift + Arrows also works.

    Z Teleport up to 6 meters where you look / 16 Ki / 2s cooldown. Stay inside the arena.

    X Pick up the highlighted nearby object / 10 Ki. Tap again to throw where you look. Pause drops it safely. With no object nearby, X fires a blast.

    C Hold guard / descend while flying

    Mouse Click the arena to look freely. Left / right click strikes; J / K also attack. Esc releases the pointer and pauses.

    L Tap for a blast: 8 Ki. Hold 0.35 seconds for a beam: 35 Ki, or 1.2 seconds for an ultimate: 100 Ki, then release. The on-screen Ki blast button works the same way. U and V fire a beam or ultimate directly.

    X / J in clash Tap to boost. Time the cue; holding or key repeat earns no extra taps. Regular energy attacks are suspended during the clash.

    C / Primary pointer in clash Hold to brace instead. Release, pause, blur or clash end clears the commitment.

    F Signature technique

    Space Toggle flight. E rises; C descends. Press Space again to land.

    T Hold to charge ki. Charge never earns resolve.

    R Transform when requirements are met

    V Ultimate / 100 ki

    H / Tab Next nearby living target and recenter. The locked target is not the only opponent in the Pit.

    1 / 2 / 3 / 4 Arena / third-person / tactical / Eye camera

    Arrows / LOOK pad Free look. Home recenters.

    Optional chains: J, J, K launcher; evade a real hit, then counter. KI pays for attacks and boosts; RESOLVE is combat-earned progress toward a form. Real hits earn resolve; melee hits also return ki. Shots still spend ki. Front guard or perfect evade returns defensive ki only against an incoming hit. Queue the next hit during recovery. Aliases: J light, K heavy, L ki, U beam, I ultimate. In flight hold E to rise. All combat buttons are clickable; touch uses the same hold and double-tap behavior.
