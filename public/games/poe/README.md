# Poe — A Small Theatre of Unease

An interactive film anthology around Arnav's recovered Poe-inspired movies. This release includes the original 65-second Masque of the Red Death film and two five-second moving studies, The House of Usher and The Raven. It is not a recovery of a branching Poe game.

Existing media was recovered from the creator's local Higgsfield Poe project on 29 September 2026. No new generation or paid provider call was used. Films were web-compressed to H.264/AAC at their existing 1280×720 resolution with fast-start metadata; cover images are actual frames from those films. Local source movies were preserved.

Film concepts and assembly by Arnav, inspired by Edgar Allan Poe. Imagery generated with Higgsfield / Kling. New anthology player assembled with Codex. The source imagery retains the creator's rights and applicable generation-provider terms; no blanket MIT license or commercial asset license is asserted for the film content.

The player uses native browser video with no login, external runtime dependencies, analytics or uploads. Choose a film, play/pause, scrub, replay, mute, or let chance choose another film. Space pauses, M toggles sound, and the arrow keys seek. The player pauses when its document is hidden.

Validation: all three videos decoded and inspected with ffprobe; duration and resolution verified; JavaScript syntax and static resource references checked. Browser interaction and human review remain separate checks.
