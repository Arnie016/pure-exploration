Where a match begins

Six entry points. Three objectives.

Each team has Delta, West and East launch clusters. Each cluster gives a five-pilot squad room to spread out. The match seed picks the opening cluster. After a disable, it tries to use one at least 36 metres from active enemies; if none qualify, it picks the farthest one.
