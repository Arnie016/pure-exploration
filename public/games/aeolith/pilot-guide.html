AEOLITH pilot practice guide

This guide suggests drills, not guaranteed winning tactics. Match totals show what was recorded, not why a team won.

covered-approach

Choose a route with terrain cover before approaching the active objective. Pause safely to check the capture indicator and nearby opponents.

clear-shot

Align the reticle with a clear firing lane before spending an ability. Practise braking and tracking a moving target without abandoning cover.

safe-exit

Choose an exit route before committing to a contest. Break line of sight when pressured and check your hull before returning.

These are practice suggestions for AEOLITH: Meridian Clash. Try them in AI practice before a human match. No upgrade guarantees an advantage.
