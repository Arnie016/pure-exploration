# Galevein: Stormflight — Ember Wyrm edition

A same-origin bundle of the current creator-owned game source, with one documented CC0 mount and original procedural scenery/audio. Original checkout preserved. W/S speed, arrows climb/dive, A/D bank/turn, Space flap, hold X for plasma, Q/E look, V first-person and M mute. Follow the beacons.

This edition uses the existing Quaternius dragon rig and its flying animation. The default Stormcrest and other large dragon variants were excluded because the current local audit has no provenance record for them. Indeterminate Fab records and assets were excluded. The Suno menu song was replaced with the project's oscillator-composed exploration theme because its generation-plan rights were unverified.

Dragon: Quaternius, Animated Monster Pack, CC0 1.0, https://quaternius.com/packs/animatedmonster.html . Pack license retained under licensed-assets/licenses. Game's existing material tint retained. The documented CC0 flight rig is normalized from its measured 6.063-unit animated wingspan to 14 world units; idle, start and reset share this scale. The chase camera retains at least 38 units of separation and widens its distance on narrow viewports to keep the full wingspan near 20% of the view. The authored CC0 animation is preserved: the legacy additive skeleton layer and mismatched giant rider are excluded for this rig, and its mouth effect compensates for the armature’s 100× bone scale. Three.js r165 MIT and Draco Apache-2.0 notices retained under licenses. Fonts: Grenze (Omnibus-Type), IM FELL English (Igino Marini), Uncial Antiqua (Astigmatic), Noto Sans Runic (Google Noto); SIL OFL notices retained. Music and effects are the project's procedural oscillator/noise compositions; no samples or new generation used. No blanket license is asserted for the creator's game code.

The existing procedural landmarks, world map, chapters, weather, storm audio and flight simulation are preserved. This is a distinct lightweight mount edition, not a claim that the other source assets are cleared.
